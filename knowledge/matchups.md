# Lane counters from pro lane records

> Generated by `tools/check_matchups.py` from `matchups_source.md` (edit that file, then rerun). Every counter carries its pro lane record (counter's W-L vs that champion in the same role, 2025-26 pro and ERL games, plus Master+ soloQ lane win rates where shown; the grade weights recent patches and major leagues more, and soloQ counts as at most 12 pseudo pro games so it mainly fills in rare pro matchups) and a grade: **strong**, **favoured**, **untested** (theory only, under 3 pro games), **even**, **doubtful** (losing in a small sample). Counters the pro data contradicts are moved to "Listed but not backed by pro data" and do not count. A counter is a range, not a switch: weigh the grade, whether the player is comfortable on it, and what it adds to the team comp. In this league a counter the player is uncomfortable on is usually worse than their comfort pick.


Generated by `tools/build_matchups_from_pro.py` from `pro_data/lane_h2h.json` only: a counter has 20+ pro games in that lane (15+ weighted) and a smoothed win rate of 0.57+. No hand-written counters. `tools/check_matchups.py` grades them into `matchups.md`.
