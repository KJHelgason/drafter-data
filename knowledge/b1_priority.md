# First-pick (B1) priority in pro play, by game of the series

From 671 pro games in 269 series (patches 26.16-26.20; `pro_data/build_b1.py`). Hard fearless: later games lose every champion played earlier, so their B1 lists are what is left.

**B1 when available** = how often pros take it B1 in games where it survived phase-1 bans (and fearless). **Phase-1 ban** = share of games it was banned in the first 3 bans of either team. **Rotation 1** = picked B1, R1 or R2.
